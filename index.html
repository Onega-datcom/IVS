Upload your valid ID

Supported IDs: .

Capture the front of your ID with your camera, or choose a JPG/PNG image (up to 5 MB).

  • Place the ID flat on a plain surface in good light.
  • Keep the whole card in the photo, with no glare or blur; move closer so the text is readable.
  • Use a sample or synthetic ID when demonstrating.

The photo is read inside your browser and never uploaded. Only the extracted text and a face template are saved.

Register and match your face

Follow the live checks: look straight, blink when asked, then move farther and closer.

  • Keep one face centered in the guide and follow each instruction.
  • Keep your entire face visible: no cap, mask or hand in front.
  • Use even lighting, with no strong light behind you. Each action is checked before moving on.
Press Start capture when you are ready.
